Source Boundaries

  • Public data only. No ownership/title lookup.
  • Permit records are signals, not full project truth.
  • Traffic-study records show where counts/speed studies exist; they do not prove a street is safe or unsafe.
  • Service requests and work orders are operational records; some dates may reflect future scheduling or stale closure state.
  • Flood services found so far are token-gated, so flood is not live-mapped here yet.
  • OSM tiles provide map context; HRM endpoints provide civic data.
